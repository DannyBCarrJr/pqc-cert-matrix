hand-edit. Captures come from `phase3/transport.py`, decrypted with a TLS
key log so every handshake message can be attributed. Server and client are
both OpenSSL 3.5.5. Raw captures in `phase3/evidence/<chain>/`.

## Computed: does the server's first flight fit the initial congestion window?

Segments are measured flight bytes divided by MSS, against an initial
congestion window of 10 segments (RFC 6928). **Computed, not measured:**
see the note in `report.py` about segmentation offload on this host.
A flight that exceeds the window waits for the client to acknowledge the
first burst before the rest can be sent, which costs an extra round trip
before the handshake completes.

| Chain | Flight bytes | Segments @ 1460 | Segments @ 1360 | Fits IW10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ecdsa | 2,244 | 2 | 2 | yes |
| catalyst | 7,584 | 6 | 6 | yes |
| mldsa44 | 11,792 | 9 | 9 | yes |
| mixed | 12,491 | 9 | 10 | yes |
| mldsa65 | 15,739 | 11 | 12 | no |
| slhroot | 20,286 | 14 | 15 | no |
| mldsa87 | 20,973 | 15 | 16 | no |

At the standard 1460-byte MSS the window holds 14,600 bytes. The heaviest chain that still fits is **mixed** at 12,491 bytes; everything above it pays an extra round trip on every full handshake.
